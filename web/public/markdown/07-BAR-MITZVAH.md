# 07 — Family Celebration Day

**Date:** Saturday, October 10, 2026
**Location:** Tokyo Dome City
**Goal:** a personal, playful celebration for the 12-year-old—not another packed sightseeing day.

## Why Tokyo Dome City Wins

- It matches his gaming/ride interests.
- The family can stay in one compact area.
- Rides, karaoke, JUMP SHOP, bowling/arcade and dinner can flex around weather.
- It avoids crossing Tokyo between unrelated “special” activities.
- It feels celebratory without forcing a formal event the child may not want.

## The Day

### Slow morning

- Sleep in.
- Have a proper brunch.
- Give the family gift privately.
- Let him choose the first ride/activity.

### Afternoon headline

At **Tokyo Dome City Attractions**:

1. Thunder Dolphin for anyone who wants it and meets the current restrictions.
2. Big-O Ferris wheel; request/choose a karaoke gondola if available.
3. One JUMP SHOP keepsake with a pre-agreed budget.
4. Bowling or arcade only if the weather affects rides or the family wants more play.

Check live ride operations and maintenance on the [official attractions site](https://www.at-raku.com/).

The Miyamoto bachi bought after Taiko-kan on Oct 16 is a separate, personal drumming keepsake. It complements this celebration day; it does not need to replace the family gift.

### Evening

- Reserve a restaurant near Tokyo Dome, Kagurazaka or the accommodation.
- Ask in advance for a dessert message.
- Keep a short private family moment: each person says one thing they admire about him and one wish for the year ahead.

## What Not to Add

- No cross-city viewpoint.
- No second major museum.
- No shopping district.
- No Tokyo Yosakoi preview. The next morning starts with an early Kyoto transfer.
- No formal ceremony unless the son specifically requests one.

## Weather Versions

### Dry

Rides → Big-O → JUMP SHOP → dinner.

### Wet

Indoor game/bowling block → Big-O if operating → JUMP SHOP → early dinner or private karaoke.

## Booking Checklist

- [ ] Ask the son privately whether he prefers rides, bowling or karaoke as the headline.
- [ ] Reserve dinner.
- [ ] Request a dessert message and confirm any dietary needs.
- [ ] Buy/wrap the family gift before travel.
- [ ] Recheck attraction maintenance one week before.
- [ ] Keep the morning empty in the calendar.

## Budget Envelope

| Item | Planning range |
|---|---:|
| Attractions / rides for four | JPY 10,000–20,000 |
| JUMP SHOP keepsake | JPY 3,000–8,000 |
| Celebration dinner | JPY 16,000–32,000 |
| Dessert/message or karaoke extra | JPY 3,000–8,000 |
| **Day envelope excluding gift** | **JPY 32,000–68,000** |

The emotional target is simple: he gets a day that feels chosen for him, the sister still has fun, and nobody is exhausted before the early Kyoto train, Mizuekai and Awata the next morning.
