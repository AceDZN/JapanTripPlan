## 1. Airport Transfers (Narita)

The fastest dedicated airport train. Runs from Narita Airport to Keisei Ueno Station (41 min) and Nippori Station (36 min).

| Online advance purchase (one-way) | 2,200 yen | 8,800 yen |
| Online advance purchase (round-trip) | 4,300 yen | 17,200 yen |

**Skyliner + Tokyo Subway Combo Ticket** (best value for arrival day):

| Combo Option | Adult Price | x4 Family |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| One-way Skyliner + 24h Subway | 2,900 yen | 11,600 yen |

- **Tip**: Buy the one-way Skyliner + 72h Subway combo on arrival (3,600 yen/person). Use a separate 72h ticket for departure week.

### Option B: Narita Express (N'EX)

JR East's airport express. Goes directly to Tokyo Station (53 min), Shinagawa, Shibuya, Shinjuku, and Yokohama -- no transfers needed for west Tokyo hotels.

| Item | Price per person | Family of 4 |
|------|-----------------|-------------|

- **Best for**: Families staying in Shinjuku/Shibuya/Ikebukuro (direct service, no transfer with luggage)

### Option C: Keisei Access Express (Budget)

Same Keisei line, but a limited express (not the premium Skyliner). Significantly cheaper.

- **Duration**: ~55-70 minutes to Asakusa/Ueno
- **Downside**: No reserved seats, can be crowded, less luggage space
- **Best for**: Budget-conscious families comfortable with luggage on a regular train

### Option D: Airport Limousine Bus

Direct bus service to major hotels and stations.

| Item | Price per person | Family of 4 |
|------|-----------------|-------------|
| One-way to central Tokyo | 3,200-3,600 yen | 12,800-14,400 yen |

- **Duration**: 75-120 min depending on traffic and destination
- **Book**: [Limousine Bus](https://www.limousinebus.co.jp/en/)
- **Best for**: Hotels with a direct bus stop (door-to-door, luggage handled for you)

### Recommendation for Your Family

**Arrival**: Skyliner + 72h Subway combo (3,600 yen x 4 = 14,400 yen) -- fast, comfortable, and includes your first 3 days of subway.

**Departure**: Skyliner online advance purchase (2,200 yen x 4 = 8,800 yen) or N'EX if your hotel is in west Tokyo.

**Airport transfer total estimate: ~23,200 yen**

## 2. IC Cards -- Suica/Pasmo Setup

IC cards are contactless smart cards used to tap in/out on virtually all trains, subways, and buses in Tokyo (and most of Japan). They also work at convenience stores, vending machines, lockers, and many restaurants.

### Option A: Welcome Suica Mobile App (Recommended for iPhone users)

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Validity | 180 days from issue |
| Deposit | None |
| Setup | Download app, add to Apple Wallet, load via Apple Pay |
| Can set up before arrival | Yes |
| Minimum charge | 1,000 yen |
| Refundable balance | No (use it up before leaving) |

- **Download**: Search "Welcome Suica" in the App Store
- **Requirements**: iPhone 8 or later, or Apple Watch Series 3 or later
- **Tip**: Set up all 4 family member cards on respective phones before your flight. Load 3,000-5,000 yen each to start.

### Option B: Physical Welcome Suica Card

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Validity | 28 days from purchase |
| Deposit | None |
| Where to buy | JR East Travel Service Centers at Narita/Haneda airports, Tokyo/Ueno/Shibuya/Shinagawa stations |
| Refundable | No |

- **Best for**: Family members without iPhones or children who want their own physical card
- **Note**: 28-day validity is fine for your 17-day trip

### Option C: Regular Suica/Pasmo Card

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Deposit | 500 yen (refundable) |
| Where to buy | Station ticket machines (Suica at JR, Pasmo at Metro/Toei) |
| Refundable balance | Yes (220 yen fee) |

- As of 2025, physical Suica cards are once again available at JR station machines after years of chip shortage.
- **Info**: [Japan Guide - IC Cards](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e2359_003.html)

### How Much to Load

For days when you are NOT using a subway pass, budget approximately 800-1,500 yen per person per day for subway/train rides. Start with 5,000 yen per card and top up as needed at any station machine or convenience store.

## 3. Tokyo Subway Passes

The **Tokyo Subway Ticket** provides unlimited rides on all 13 Tokyo Metro lines AND all 4 Toei Subway lines. This is the best value for heavy sightseeing days.

### Tokyo Subway Ticket Prices

| Duration | Adult (12+) | Child (6-11) | Your Family of 4 |
|----------|------------|-------------|-------------------|
| 24-hour | 800 yen | 400 yen | 3,200 yen |
| 48-hour | 1,200 yen | 600 yen | 4,800 yen |
| 72-hour | 1,500 yen | 750 yen | 6,000 yen |

- **Note**: All 4 of your family members pay adult fare (both kids are 12+)
- **Validity**: Consecutive hours from first use (not calendar days)
- **Purchase locations**: Narita/Haneda airports, BIC Camera stores, select hotels, or online via [Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/1552-subway-ticket-tokyo/)
- **Passport required** at purchase
- **Official info**: [Tokyo Metro Subway Ticket](https://www.tokyometro.jp/en/ticket/travel/index.html)

### Tokyo Metro 24-Hour Ticket (Metro Only)

| Duration | Adult | Child (6-11) |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| 24-hour | 700 yen | 350 yen |

- Covers Tokyo Metro lines only (not Toei). Less useful since many attractions require Toei lines too.

### Strategy: When to Use Passes vs. IC Card

| Scenario | Best Option | Why |
|----------|------------|-----|
| Heavy sightseeing day (4+ rides) | 24h Subway Ticket | Breaks even at ~4 rides |
| Light day (1-2 rides + walking) | IC Card (Suica) | Pay-as-you-go is cheaper |
| First 3 days (jet lag sightseeing) | 72h Subway Ticket | Best per-day rate (500 yen/day) |
| Day trips out of Tokyo | IC Card | Passes don't cover JR or private lines |

### Recommended Pass Strategy for 17 Days

| Period | Days | Method | Cost per person | Family x4 |
|--------|------|--------|----------------|-----------|
| Days 1-3 (arrive, explore) | 3 | 72h Subway Ticket | 1,500 yen | 6,000 yen |
| Days 4-6 | 3 | IC Card (~1,000/day) | ~3,000 yen | ~12,000 yen |
| Days 7-9 (heavy sightseeing) | 3 | 72h Subway Ticket | 1,500 yen | 6,000 yen |
| Days 10-12 | 3 | IC Card (~1,000/day) | ~3,000 yen | ~12,000 yen |
| Days 13-15 | 3 | 72h Subway Ticket or IC | ~1,500-3,000 yen | ~6,000-12,000 yen |
| Days 16-17 (wind down, depart) | 2 | IC Card (~800/day) | ~1,600 yen | ~6,400 yen |

**Estimated subway/metro total: ~12,000-15,000 yen per person / ~48,000-60,000 yen family**

Realistically, with a mix of passes and IC card days, budget **~13,000 yen/person** for in-city transit over 17 days.

## 5. Day Trip Transport

### A. Hakone (Recommended: 1-2 days)

**Hakone Free Pass** -- includes round-trip from Shinjuku + unlimited Hakone transport (bus, ropeway, cable car, pirate ship, Tozan Railway).

| Pass | Adult Price | Family of 4 |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| 2-day Hakone Free Pass (from Shinjuku) | 6,100 yen | 24,400 yen |
| 3-day Hakone Free Pass (from Shinjuku) | 6,500 yen | 26,000 yen |

- **Note**: No 1-day pass exists. Valid for consecutive calendar days (not 24h periods).
- **Worth it?** Individual transport within Hakone costs ~8,170 yen, so the pass saves ~2,000 yen per person.
- **Upgrade**: Add Romancecar limited express for ~1,110 yen each way (reserved seat, more comfortable, scenic).
- **Purchase**: Odakyu Sightseeing Service Center at Shinjuku Station or [online via Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/821-hakone-transport-day-pass-tokyo/)
- **Official info**: [Odakyu Hakone Freepass](https://odakyu-global.com/passes/hakone-freepass/)

### B. Kamakura (1 day)

| Option | Adult Price (one-way) | Round Trip x4 |
|--------|---------------------|---------------|
| JR Yokosuka Line (Tokyo Stn -> Kamakura) | 950 yen | 7,600 yen |
| JR Shonan-Shinjuku Line (Shinjuku -> Kamakura) | 950 yen | 7,600 yen |
| Enoshima-Kamakura Free Pass (from Shinjuku) | 1,640 yen (incl. round trip + Enoden) | 6,560 yen |

- **JR route**: ~55-60 min, direct, no transfers. Best for speed.
- **Enoshima-Kamakura Free Pass**: Includes round trip on Odakyu + unlimited Enoden tram (scenic coastal line). Takes longer (~90 min) but saves money and the Enoden ride is a highlight.
- **Within Kamakura**: Walk or use Enoden tram (190-310 yen per ride, or free with Free Pass).
- **Official info**: [Japan Guide - Kamakura Access](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3115.html)

### C. Nikko (1 day)

**Nikko World Heritage Area Pass** (Tobu Railway) -- best value for a Nikko day trip.

| Pass | Adult Price | Family of 4 |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| 2-day World Heritage Area Pass | 3,000 yen | 12,000 yen |
| 4-day All Area Pass | 8,000 yen | 32,000 yen |

- **Includes**: Round-trip Tobu Railway (Asakusa -> Nikko) + unlimited bus in the shrine area
- **Does NOT include**: Limited express surcharge (1,450-2,140 yen each way if you want the faster Spacia train)
- **Without limited express**: ~2 hours each way on regular Tobu trains
- **With limited express**: ~1h50 each way (Spacia X or Spacia)
- **Passport required** for purchase (foreign tourist only)
- **Purchase**: Tobu Asakusa Station tourist counter
- **Official info**: [Tobu Nikko Pass](https://www.tobu.co.jp/en/ticket/nikko/city.html)

### D. Mount Fuji / Kawaguchiko (1 day)

| Option | Adult Price (one-way) | Round Trip x4 |
|--------|---------------------|---------------|
| Fuji Excursion train (Shinjuku -> Kawaguchiko) | 4,130 yen | 33,040 yen |
| JR + Fujikyu transfer (Shinjuku -> Otsuki -> Kawaguchiko) | ~2,510 yen | ~20,080 yen |
| Highway bus (Shinjuku -> Kawaguchiko) | ~2,000-2,200 yen | ~16,000-17,600 yen |

- **Fuji Excursion**: Direct, reserved seats, scenic, ~2 hours. Runs on weekdays and weekends Oct 2026. Book early as it sells out.
- **Bus**: Cheapest option, ~2-2.5 hours, frequent departures from Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal.
- **Book Fuji Excursion**: [Fujikyu Railway](https://e.fujikyu-railway.jp/fujikaiyuu/) or [Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/japan-rail/fuji-excursion/)
- **Book bus**: [Highway Bus](https://highway-buses.jp/fuji/)

### E. JR Tokyo Wide Pass (for multiple day trips)

If you plan 2+ day trips using JR lines (Kamakura, Nikko via JR, Kawaguchiko via Fuji Excursion), consider:

| Pass | Price | Validity |
|------|-------|----------|
| JR Tokyo Wide Pass | 15,000 yen/person | 3 consecutive days |

- Covers Shinkansen and limited express in the greater Tokyo area (including Nikko, Kawaguchiko, Karuizawa, Izu)
- **Worth it if**: You do 3 day trips in 3 consecutive days (unlikely with a family)
- **Not worth it for**: Casual day-tripping spread across the trip
- **Foreign passport required**
- **Official info**: [JR East Tokyo Wide Pass](https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/multi/pass/tokyowidepass.html)

### Full Japan Rail Pass -- Probably NOT Worth It

For a Tokyo-only trip with day trips, the nationwide JR Pass (50,000 yen/7 days adult) is almost certainly not worth it. It only pays off for long-distance Shinkansen travel (Tokyo-Kyoto-Osaka-Hiroshima type itineraries).

## 6. Children's Fare Rules Reference

This is critical for budgeting. Japan's train fare system categorizes passengers by age:

| Category | Age | Fare Rule |
|----------|-----|-----------|
| Adult | 12+ (or in junior high school) | Full fare |
| Child | 6-11 (elementary school) | Half fare (rounded up to nearest 10 yen) |
| Young child | 1-5 | Free (with paying passenger, max 2 free per adult) |
| Infant | Under 1 | Free |

### What This Means for Your Family

| Family Member | Age | Fare Category |
|--------------|-----|---------------|
| Parent 1 | Adult | Full adult fare |
| Parent 2 | Adult | Full adult fare |
| Daughter | 16 | Full adult fare |
| Son | 12 | Full adult fare |

**All 4 family members pay full adult fare on all transport.**

The 12-year-old threshold applies even if the child is still in elementary school grade 6 -- once they turn 12, they pay adult fare. Since your son is 12 during the trip, he pays adult fare.

Sources:
- [JR Central - Adults and Children](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/tickets/type/adults_children.html)
- [Tokyo Metro - Fares by Age](https://www.tokyometro.jp/en/ticket/age/index.html)

## 8. Practical Tips

### Before You Leave Home

- [ ] Download **Welcome Suica** app on all iPhones and set up cards
- [ ] Load 5,000 yen per card via Apple Pay
- [ ] Book Skyliner tickets online at [Keisei e-ticket](https://www.keisei.co.jp/keisei/tetudou/skyliner/e-ticket/en/) (saves ~300 yen/person vs. counter)
- [ ] Download **Japan Travel by NAVITIME** app (offline transit navigation)
- [ ] Download **Google Maps** offline maps for Tokyo metro area

### At Narita Airport on Arrival

1. Clear immigration and customs
2. Pick up pocket WiFi / activate eSIM (if pre-ordered)
3. If using physical IC cards: buy Welcome Suica at JR East Travel Service Center (near Skyliner ticket counter)
4. Buy Skyliner + 72h Subway combo ticket at Keisei counter (or show e-ticket)
5. Activate 72h subway ticket on first Metro/Toei ride (not the Skyliner ride)

### Daily Transit Tips

- **Rush hours to avoid**: 7:30-9:30 AM and 5:30-8:00 PM on weekdays. Trains are extremely crowded.
- **IC card top-up**: Available at every station (machines accept cash and some accept credit cards). Convenience stores (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart) also do top-ups.
- **Transfers**: When transferring between Metro and Toei lines at the same station, you get a discount (~70 yen) -- just tap through normally with IC card.
- **Last trains**: Most lines end around 12:00-12:30 AM. Plan accordingly or budget for a taxi (~3,000-5,000 yen for short rides).
- **Priority seats**: Always available near the ends of each car. Let elderly, pregnant, and disabled passengers use them.
- **Quiet cars**: Shinkansen and some limited express trains have designated quiet cars. Keep volume down.
- **Luggage**: Trains have no dedicated luggage areas. Avoid rush hour with suitcases. For day trips, use coin lockers at the station (300-700 yen depending on size).
