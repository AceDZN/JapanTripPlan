## Detailed Airline Comparison

### 1. Ethiopian Airlines (Best Price)

- **Route:** TLV -> Addis Ababa (ADD) -> Tokyo Narita (NRT)
- **Price Range:** ~$841-900 per person round-trip
- **Family of 4:** ~$3,360-3,600 USD (~12,100-13,000 NIS)
- **Frequency:** 16 connecting flights per week
- **Flight Duration:** 16-20 hours total (depending on layover)
- **Aircraft:** Mix of Boeing 787 Dreamliner and Airbus A350
- **Stopover Perk:** Free hotel stay for layovers of 8-24 hours in Addis Ababa, including meals
- **Book:** [Ethiopian Airlines](https://www.ethiopianairlines.com/en-il/flights-from-tel-aviv-to-tokyo)

**Pros:**
- Consistently the cheapest option from TLV to Tokyo
- Free hotel on long layovers (8-24 hours)
- Modern fleet with good entertainment
- Star Alliance member (earn miles on United, ANA, etc.)

**Cons:**
- Longer total journey time
- Addis Ababa connection less convenient than Gulf hubs
- Some layovers can be very long (6-12 hours)
- Less frequent service compared to Etihad

### 2. Etihad Airways (Best Value with Stopover)

- **Route:** TLV -> Abu Dhabi (AUH) -> Tokyo Narita (NRT)
- **Price Range:** ~$900-1,000 per person round-trip (from ~$716 in off-peak)
- **Family of 4:** ~$3,600-4,000 USD (~13,000-14,400 NIS)
- **Frequency:** 28 connecting flights per week (most frequent option)
- **Fastest Connection:** ~15 hours 25 minutes
- **Stopover Perk:** Up to 2 FREE hotel nights in Abu Dhabi via Etihad Stopover Programme
- **Book:** [Etihad Airways](https://www.etihad.com/en/flights/flights-from-tel-aviv-to-tokyo)

**Pros:**
- Highest flight frequency (28/week = 4 per day)
- Free 2-night Abu Dhabi stopover -- turn the connection into a mini-vacation
- Family-friendly: priority boarding, child meals, activity packs, onboard entertainment
- Competitive pricing, only slightly more than Ethiopian
- Modern fleet, excellent lounge in Abu Dhabi

**Cons:**
- Not the absolute cheapest
- Abu Dhabi stopover adds travel days (great if you want it, less so if not)
- Not a major alliance member (limited partner airlines for miles)

**Stopover Details:**
The Abu Dhabi Stopover Programme lets you break up your journey with up to 2 complimentary hotel nights. Perfect for a family wanting to break up the long journey and visit attractions like Yas Island, Ferrari World, or the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque.

### 3. Turkish Airlines (Great Stopover City)

- **Route:** TLV -> Istanbul (IST) -> Tokyo Narita (NRT)
- **Price Range:** ~$900-1,100 per person round-trip
- **Family of 4:** ~$3,600-4,400 USD (~13,000-15,800 NIS)
- **Flight Duration:** 16-19 hours total
- **Stopover Perk:** **Most generous free stopover program** -- up to 2 nights FREE at 4-star hotels (Economy) or 3 nights at 5-star hotels like Conrad/Hilton (Business)
- **Book:** [Turkish Airlines](https://www.turkishairlines.com/en-us/flights/booking)

**Pros:**
- Istanbul is an incredible stopover city (history, food, culture)
- Most generous hotel stopover program in the industry
- Excellent in-flight catering (Turkish cuisine)
- Star Alliance member (earn/redeem miles with United, ANA, Lufthansa)
- TLV-IST is a very short first leg (~2 hours)

**Cons:**
- October can be slightly pricier than other Gulf carriers
- Must apply for free hotel at least 72 hours before departure
- Stopovers can be extended up to 7 days maximum
- Istanbul airport (IST) is massive -- allow time for connections

**Stopover Details:**
Apply at least 72 hours before departure through the Turkish Stopover page. Economy passengers get 2 nights at 4-star hotels. This is arguably the best stopover deal in aviation.

| **August 2026 (60-70 days out)** | Optimal for fall travel | Best time for October flights |
| **September 2026 (30 days out)** | Prices rising | Last chance for decent fares |

**Key insight:** The optimal booking window for October flights to Japan is **60-90 days in advance** (July-August 2026). This is when you get the best balance of price and availability.

### Day-of-Week Strategy

- **Book on:** Tuesday, Wednesday, or Sunday (6-13% cheaper than Friday)
- **Book at:** 8:00 PM - 11:00 PM local time (midweek evenings yield better deals)
- **Fly on:** Tuesday-Thursday for lowest fares (avoid Friday/Sunday departures)

### October-Specific Considerations

- October is **not peak season** for Japan flights from Israel -- this works in your favor
- However, China's National Golden Week (Oct 1-7) can push up Asia-bound flight prices early October
- Late October coincides with fall foliage season -- expect 15-25% above baseline pricing
- **Tip:** If flexible, departing October 2-3 instead of October 1 may save money (avoiding Golden Week surge)

## Research Notes

- **Data collected:** February 8, 2026
- **Prices are estimates** based on current fares and seasonal patterns. Actual October 2026 prices will vary.
- **All prices are per person in economy class** unless otherwise noted. Multiply by 4 for family total.
- **Exchange rate used:** 1 USD = ~3.6 NIS (approximate, check current rate when booking)
- **Children's fares:** Most airlines offer the same fare for children aged 2-11 occupying a seat. Infants under 2 fly for ~10% of adult fare (lap) or ~75% (own seat). The input specifies 4 adults, so full adult fares apply.
- **Baggage:** All listed airlines include at least 1x23kg checked bag in economy on long-haul international flights. Verify at time of booking.

> **Bottom line for the family budget:** Target spending **$3,400-4,000 USD (12,200-14,400 NIS)** on flights for all 4 passengers by booking with Etihad or Ethiopian in July 2026.
