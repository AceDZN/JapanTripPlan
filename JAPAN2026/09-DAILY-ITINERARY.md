## DAY 2 -- Friday, October 2: UENO & ASAKUSA (Neighborhood Orientation)

**Theme:** Explore your home base + iconic Asakusa
**Holiday:** Sukkot
**Walking Distance:** ~6 km total

### Schedule

**Morning (08:00-12:00) -- Ueno Park & Museums**
- **Breakfast:** Konbini near hotel (onigiri, pastries, coffee ~1,500)
- Walk to **Ueno Park** (5 min from most Ueno hotels)
  - Stroll through the park, visit **Shinobazu Pond** (free)
  - **Tokyo National Museum** (oldest/largest museum in Japan)
    - Adults: 1,000 | Under 18: FREE
    - Allow 1.5-2 hours
    - Nearest station: JR Ueno (Park Exit)
  - Alternative: **National Museum of Nature and Science** (great for kids)
    - Adults: 630 | Under 18: FREE
    - Has dinosaur skeletons, space exhibits, cool interactive displays
- Visit **Ueno Tosho-gu Shrine** inside the park (free to enter grounds)

**Afternoon (12:30-17:00) -- Asakusa**
- Walk from Ueno to Asakusa (~20 min pleasant walk) or take Tokyo Metro Ginza Line (Ueno to Asakusa, 5 min, ~170/person)
- **Lunch at Asakusa:** Try **Sometaro** (okonomiyaki, you cook it yourself -- kids love it, ~1,000/person) or grab street food along Nakamise-dori
- **Senso-ji Temple** (FREE)
  - Enter through **Kaminarimon Gate** (Thunder Gate) -- iconic photo spot
  - Walk **Nakamise-dori** shopping street (buy small souvenirs, snacks, ningyo-yaki)
  - Explore the temple grounds, incense ritual, fortune papers (omikuji, 100 each)
  - Allow 1-1.5 hours
- **Tokyo Skytree** (visible from Asakusa, ~15 min walk)
  - Tembo Deck (350m): Adults 2,100 | Child (6-11) 950 | Child (12-17) 1,550
  - Tembo Galleria (450m): additional Adults 1,000 | Child 450 | Child 750
  - **Pokemon Center Skytree Town** is at the base! (FREE to browse)
    - First Pokemon Center visit -- let the son explore
  - Tip: Go up Skytree late afternoon for daylight AND sunset views

**Evening (17:30-20:00)**
- Explore **Skytree Solamachi Mall** (shops and restaurants at the base)
- **Dinner:** Solamachi has many affordable restaurants on floors 6-7
  - Try **Rokurinsya** for tsukemen (dipping ramen, ~1,000/person)
  - Or eat at one of the family-friendly sets restaurants
- Walk back to Ueno along the Sumida River (beautiful evening walk, ~25 min)
- Consider visiting **Chabad of Tokyo** for Sukkot if desired
  - Chabad Tokyo: Roppongi area (Nishi-Azabu) -- would need to take Hibiya Line from Ueno

### Logistics
| Item | Cost (family of 4) |
|------|-------------------|
| Breakfast (konbini) | ~1,500 |
| Museum (1 adult + 1 adult, kids free) | ~2,000 |
| Transport (walking + Metro) | ~1,500 |

- Senso-ji is stunning but gets VERY crowded by 11:00. You're going in the afternoon, which is fine -- the crowd thins by 15:00.

- Pokemon Center Skytree is smaller than the Mega centers -- save serious shopping for Shibuya or Ikebukuro.
- Asakusa has excellent street food: melon pan, ningyo-yaki, age-manju (fried manju).

## DAY 3 -- Saturday, October 3: AKIHABARA -- ANIME & ELECTRONICS PARADISE

**Theme:** Full anime/gaming/electronics immersion
**Holiday:** Sukkot / Shabbat
**Nearest Station:** JR Akihabara (2 stops from Ueno on JR Yamanote/Keihin-Tohoku Line)

### Schedule

**Morning (09:30-12:30) -- Akihabara North Side**
- **Breakfast:** Konbini or try a Japanese-style breakfast at hotel
- Train from Ueno to Akihabara (4 min, ~140/person)
- Start at **Yodobashi Camera Akihabara** (opens 09:30)
  - Massive 9-floor electronics store. Cameras, gadgets, Japanese electronics
  - Tax-free shopping for tourists (bring passport)
  - Budget tip: look, compare prices, but don't buy impulsively -- you have 14 more days
- Walk to **Radio Kaikan** (multi-floor otaku building)
  - Anime figures, trading cards, retro games
  - Each floor is a different specialty shop
- **Mandarake Akihabara** (Complex building)
  - 8 floors of used manga, figures, cosplay, doujinshi
  - This is HEAVEN for the 12-year-old. Budget warning: set a spending limit!

**Afternoon (12:30-17:30) -- Akihabara South & Electric Town**
- **Lunch:** Try a **maid cafe** for the experience (e.g., @Home Cafe or MaiDreamin)
  - ~1,500-2,500/person with food. It's quirky and the kids will find it hilarious or weird (both are valid)
  - Alternative: **CoCo Ichibanya** for curry rice (~800/person, customize spice levels)
- Walk down **Chuo-dori** (main street, pedestrian-only on Saturdays/Sundays!)
  - Browse the smaller electronics and anime shops
- **Super Potato** (retro game store)
  - Retro game paradise: NES, SNES, N64, Game Boy, Sega games
  - Top floor has playable retro arcade cabinets (100 per play)
  - The son will NOT want to leave
- **Gachapon machines** -- they're EVERYWHERE in Akihabara
  - Budget ~500-1,000 per person for gachapon (capsule toys, 200-500 each)
- **Taito Station** or **SEGA Arcade** (if still open)
  - Crane games (UFO catchers), rhythm games, photo booths (purikura)
  - Budget ~1,000-2,000 for arcade games
- **Animate Akihabara** -- large anime goods store
  - Books, figures, keychains, posters, stationery

**Evening (17:30-20:00)**
- Akihabara transforms at night with neon lights -- take photos!
- **Dinner:** **Kanda Yabu Soba** (historic soba restaurant, ~10 min walk from Akihabara)
  - One of Tokyo's oldest soba restaurants (since 1880)
  - ~1,000-1,500/person
  - Nearest station: Awajicho (or walk from Akihabara)
  - Alternative: **Gyukatsu Motomura** for beef katsu (~1,500/person)
- Walk back to Ueno (15 min) or take JR (4 min)

### Logistics
| Item | Cost (family of 4) |
|------|-------------------|
| Breakfast (konbini) | ~1,500 |
| Transport | ~1,200 |
| Lunch (maid cafe or curry) | ~5,000-8,000 |
| Arcade/gachapon | ~3,000 |
| Shopping (figures, manga, etc.) | ~5,000-15,000 (SET A LIMIT!) |
| Dinner (soba) | ~5,000 |
| **Day Total** | **~20,700-33,700** |

### Tips
- **SET A SHOPPING BUDGET** for Akihabara before you go. Seriously. It's easy to spend 50,000+ here.
- Chuo-dori is pedestrian-only on Saturdays 13:00-17:00 (and Sundays) -- perfect timing!
- Tax-free shopping requires minimum purchase of 5,000 at a single store (bring passports).
- The daughter (16) will love the aesthetic goods, stationery, and cosplay shops.
- Many stores don't open until 10:00-11:00 -- Yodobashi at 09:30 is the early bird option.
- If observing Shabbat: Akihabara exploration can be shifted to Sunday instead.

## DAY 4 -- Sunday, October 4: HARAJUKU & SHIBUYA -- POP CULTURE & FASHION

**Theme:** Youth culture, fashion, street style + Meiji Shrine
**Holiday:** Simchat Torah
**Walking Distance:** ~5 km (these areas are adjacent)

### Schedule

**Morning (09:00-12:30) -- Harajuku & Meiji Shrine**
- **Breakfast:** Konbini or try a trendy Harajuku cafe (see lunch notes)
- Train from Ueno to Harajuku (JR Yamanote Line, ~25 min, ~200/person)
- **Meiji Shrine (Meiji Jingu)** (opens at sunrise, FREE)
  - Walk through the towering torii gate and forested path
  - Beautiful, serene atmosphere -- contrast to the chaos of Harajuku outside
  - Write wishes on ema (wooden plaques, 500 each)
  - Allow 45-60 minutes
  - Nearest station: JR Harajuku (Meiji Jingumae on Metro)
- **Takeshita Street (Takeshita-dori)**
  - THE teen fashion/pop culture street
  - Crepes, cotton candy, purikura photo booths
  - Quirky fashion shops, 100-yen accessories
  - The daughter will LOVE this
  - Try a **rainbow cotton candy** or **Harajuku crepe** (~500 each)
- **Kiddy Land Harajuku** (toy store with character goods, Sanrio, etc.)
- Walk down **Cat Street** (Ura-Harajuku) -- trendier, less crowded, cool boutiques

**Afternoon (12:30-17:30) -- Shibuya**
- Walk from Harajuku to Shibuya (~10 min walk south along Meiji-dori)
- **Lunch:** **Shibuya 109 food court** (basement) or **Genki Sushi** (conveyor belt sushi near Shibuya Station, ~1,000-1,500/person)
- **Shibuya Scramble Crossing** -- the world's busiest intersection
  - Watch from above at **Shibuya Sky** observation deck (adults 2,000, children 900-1,400)
  - Or watch for free from the **Starbucks** in Tsutaya building (2nd floor, buy a drink)
  - Or from **Shibuya Hikarie** (free, 11th floor Sky Lobby)
- **Hachiko Statue** -- the faithful dog statue outside Shibuya Station (free, quick photo)
- **Pokemon Center Shibuya** (Shibuya Parco 6F)
  - This is a MEGA center -- one of the biggest
  - Nintendo Tokyo is on the same floor!
  - Allow 45-60 minutes for browsing
  - 30th Anniversary exclusive merchandise in 2026!
- **Shibuya Parco** -- explore the building (anime floor, art, fashion)
- Walk through **Shibuya Center-Gai** (pedestrian shopping street)
- **Mega Don Quijote Shibuya** (discount store -- everything from snacks to electronics to costumes)
  - Great for souvenir shopping, Japanese candy, novelty items
  - Tax-free available

**Evening (17:30-20:30)**
- **Koishikawa Korakuen Autumn Illumination** (opens Oct 10 -- NOT available yet on Oct 4)
  - Skip this evening activity; save for later in the trip
- Instead: explore Shibuya at night (the neon lights are spectacular)
- **Dinner:** **Ichiran Ramen Shibuya** (each person has their own booth -- unique experience, ~1,000/person) or **Afuri** (yuzu shio ramen, lighter style, ~1,100/person)
- Return to Ueno via JR Yamanote Line (~30 min)

## DAY 7 -- Wednesday, October 7: KAMAKURA DAY TRIP

**Theme:** Great Buddha, temples, coastal beauty, Enoshima
**Transport:** JR Yokosuka Line from Tokyo/Ueno (~60 min, ~950/person one way)

### Schedule

**Morning (07:30-12:00) -- Kamakura Temples**
- **Breakfast:** Konbini (eat on the train or before departing)
- Early departure: Ueno -> Tokyo Station (JR, 7 min) -> JR Yokosuka Line to Kamakura (~55 min)
  - Or Ueno -> Kamakura via JR Ueno-Tokyo Line directly
  - ~950/person each way
- Arrive Kamakura ~09:00
- **Tsurugaoka Hachimangu Shrine** (main shrine, 10 min walk from station)
  - Beautiful approach along Wakamiya Oji (tree-lined avenue)
  - FREE to enter grounds
  - Allow 30-45 minutes
- Walk to **Kotoku-in Temple** (Great Buddha / Daibutsu)
  - 13.35m tall bronze Buddha, outdoors -- iconic!
  - Adults 300 | Children 150
  - You can go INSIDE the Buddha for an extra 50
  - ~25 min walk from Hachimangu, or take Enoden train 1 stop
  - Allow 30-45 minutes
- **Hase-dera Temple** (5 min walk from Great Buddha)
  - Beautiful temple with gardens, cave exploration, ocean views
  - Adults 400 | Children 200
  - The cave with carved Buddha statues is fascinating for kids
  - Allow 45 minutes

**Afternoon (12:00-17:30) -- Enoshima Island**
- **Lunch in Hase area:** Try **shirasu** (tiny whitebait fish, Kamakura specialty!)
  - **Shirasu-ya** near Hase Station or any restaurant displaying "shirasu" signs
  - Shirasu-don (rice bowl with raw/boiled whitebait): ~1,000-1,500/person
- Take **Enoden train** from Hase to Enoshima (~25 min, ~310/person)
  - The Enoden is a charming old-fashioned train that runs along the coast
  - Sit on the RIGHT side for ocean views
- **Enoshima Island**
  - Walk across the bridge to the island
  - Explore the **Benzaiten Nakamise Street** (shops, snacks)
  - Climb up to **Enoshima Shrine** (free, multiple levels)
  - **Enoshima Sea Candle** (lighthouse/observation tower): 500/person
  - **Enoshima Samuel Cocking Garden**: 200/person
  - If time permits: **Iwaya Caves** (500/person) -- sea caves with Buddhist statues
  - Allow 2-3 hours for Enoshima exploration
- **Tip:** Buy the **Eno-pass** (Enoshima+Kamakura sightseeing pass) if it covers your route

**Evening (17:30-20:00)**
- Watch sunset from Enoshima (if timing works -- sunset ~17:15 in early October)
- Return: Enoshima -> (Odakyu or Enoden to Fujisawa) -> JR to Tokyo/Ueno
  - Alternative: Enoden back to Kamakura, then JR Yokosuka Line
  - ~1.5 hours total
- **Dinner:** Eat in Kamakura before departing, or grab ekiben (train station bento) for the ride
  - **Kamakura Matsubara-an** (soba near Kamakura Station, ~1,000/person)
  - Or eat near Ueno upon return

| Transport (round trip to Kamakura + Enoden) | ~9,000 |

### Tips

  - Visit **Ameyoko** for any last-minute food souvenirs
  - Quick visit to Senso-ji if you're not pressed for time

**Mid-Morning (10:00-12:00) -- Airport Transport**

**To Narita Airport (if departing from Narita):**
- **Keisei Skyliner** from Ueno (fastest, most convenient)
  - 36 minutes to Narita Terminal 1 or 2
  - ~2,520/person
  - Trains every 20-40 minutes
  - Reserve seats at Keisei Ueno Station
  - Family of 4: ~10,080
- **Alternative: Access Express** from Ueno
  - 55 minutes, ~1,270/person
  - Family of 4: ~5,080
  - No reservation needed, less legroom
- **Alternative: Narita Express (N'EX)** from Tokyo Station
  - JR Ueno to Tokyo Station (7 min), then N'EX
  - ~3,250/person to Narita
  - Round trip discount available but you only need one way
- **Arrive at airport 3 hours before international flight**
  - Budget extra time for: tax-free refund processing, last shopping, security

**To Haneda Airport (if departing from Haneda):**
- Much closer! Multiple options:
  - JR to Hamamatsucho -> Tokyo Monorail to Haneda (~45 min total, ~660/person)
  - Metro to various connections -> Keikyu Line to Haneda (~40-50 min, ~600/person)
- Arrive 2.5-3 hours before international flight

**At the Airport (12:00-departure)**
- **Tax-free processing:** Some stores seal items in bags for customs; keep them accessible
- **Last shopping at the airport!**
  - Airport duty-free shops have great Japanese souvenirs
  - **Tokyo Banana** (classic Tokyo souvenir, ~1,000-1,500 per box)
  - **Royce chocolate** (Hokkaido chocolate, available at airport, ~800-1,500)
  - **Character goods** -- airport has Pokemon, Ghibli, Sanrio shops
  - Japanese whisky and sake (duty-free)
  - **Warning:** Airport prices are slightly higher, but the selection is curated
- **Spend remaining IC card balance:**
  - Use IC cards at airport konbini or vending machines
  - IC cards can be refunded at the airport station window (get back 500 deposit minus 220 fee)
  - Or keep them as souvenirs (they're valid for 10 years!) -- good for a return trip!
- **Currency:** Spend remaining yen at airport or exchange back
  - Better to spend it (less exchange loss)
- **Relax at the gate**
  - Japan airports have excellent facilities: clean, comfortable, well-organized
  - Narita has observation decks, lounges, free WiFi, charging stations
