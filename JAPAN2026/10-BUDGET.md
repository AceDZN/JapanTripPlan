### Credit Card Tips

| Tip | Detail |
|---|---|
| Visa and Mastercard | Widely accepted at larger establishments |
| AMEX / Diners | Less accepted; bring Visa/MC as backup |

### Japan Is Still Cash-Heavy

Despite modernization, many places in Japan are **cash only**:
- Small ramen shops and local restaurants
- Temple admission fees
- Street food stalls
- Some smaller attractions
- Coin lockers
- Some taxis (though most now take IC cards)

**Rule of thumb:** Always have at least 10,000-15,000 JPY in cash on you per family.

## 8. Tax-Free Shopping

### How It Works

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | Foreign tourists on a "temporary visitor" visa stamp |
| Minimum purchase | 5,000 JPY (excl. tax) at a single store, same day |
| Tax saved | 10% consumption tax is waived |
| Categories | General goods (electronics, clothing) and consumables (food, cosmetics) |
| Passport required | Bring your passport when shopping; it will be scanned/stamped |
| Consumables rule | Must leave Japan unopened; sealed in a bag at the store |
| General goods | Must leave Japan within 6 months |

### Where to Shop Tax-Free

| Store | What to Buy | Tax-Free? |
|---|---|---|
| Don Quijote (Donki) | Everything: snacks, cosmetics, toys, souvenirs | Yes (above 5,000 yen) |
| BIC Camera | Electronics, cameras, gadgets | Yes |

| Pokemon Center | Pokemon merchandise | Yes (above 5,000 yen) |
| Matsumoto Kiyoshi | Cosmetics, medicine, snacks | Yes |

### Tax-Free Shopping Tips
