# Day Trips from Tokyo

**Trip dates:** October 1-17, 2026 | **Family of 4 from Israel** | **Base:** Tokyo

October is an excellent time for day trips -- comfortable temperatures (15-23C), early autumn foliage beginning in mountain areas, and fewer crowds than summer. Below are the top day trip destinations ranked by overall recommendation for a family.

## #1 Kamakura -- "Kyoto by the Sea"

**Why #1:** Closest major cultural destination, easy for families, combines temples with beach time, affordable, and manageable in a single day without exhaustion.

### How to Get There

| Route | Line | Duration | Cost (one way) |
|-------|------|----------|----------------|
| Tokyo Station -> Kamakura | JR Yokosuka Line (direct) | ~56 min | 920 |
| Shinjuku -> Kamakura | JR Shonan-Shinjuku Line | ~59 min | 920 |

- Trains run frequently (every 10-15 minutes)
- Covered by JR Pass if you have one
- Enoshima-Kamakura Free Pass (from Shinjuku via Odakyu) available for 1,520 for unlimited Enoden rides

### What to See

| Attraction | Admission | Time Needed | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Great Buddha (Kotoku-in) | 300 (child 150) | 45-60 min | 11.4m bronze statue, can go inside for extra 50 |
| Hasedera Temple | 400 (child 200) | 60-90 min | Gardens, jizo statues, ocean views |
| Tsurugaoka Hachimangu Shrine | Free | 45-60 min | Main shrine, impressive approach |
| Komachi-dori Street | Free | 60-90 min | Shopping street, snacks, souvenirs |
| Zeniarai Benten Shrine | Free | 30 min | Wash coins for good fortune (kids love it) |
| Enoshima Island | Free (some attractions paid) | 2-3 hours | Sea caves, lighthouse, aquarium nearby |

### Suggested Family Itinerary

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 8:00 | Depart Tokyo Station (JR Yokosuka Line) |
| 9:00 | Arrive Kamakura, walk to Tsurugaoka Hachimangu |
| 9:15-10:15 | Explore Hachimangu Shrine + grounds |
| 10:15-10:45 | Walk to Komachi-dori, snack & browse |
| 11:00 | Take Enoden train to Hase Station |
| 11:15-12:15 | Hasedera Temple |
| 12:15-13:00 | Walk to Great Buddha (Kotoku-in) |

| 16:30 | Return to Kamakura Station |
| 17:30 | Back in Tokyo |

### Estimated Cost (Family of 4, 2 adults + 2 children)

## #3 Hakone -- "Gateway to Mt Fuji"

**Why #3:** Stunning scenery, the famous "Hakone Loop" (train, cable car, ropeway, pirate ship), potential Mt Fuji views, and hot springs. More expensive but a truly memorable experience.

### How to Get There

| Route | Line | Duration | Cost (one way) |
|-------|------|----------|----------------|
| Shinjuku -> Hakone-Yumoto | Odakyu Romancecar (reserved) | ~80 min | 2,330 (incl. surcharge) |
| Shinjuku -> Hakone-Yumoto | Odakyu local/express | ~100 min | 1,260 |

**Hakone Freepass (strongly recommended):**

| Pass Type | Adult | Child (6-11) |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| 2-day Freepass (from Shinjuku) | 7,100 | 1,600 |
| 3-day Freepass (from Shinjuku) | 7,600 | 1,750 |

The Freepass includes: round-trip Shinjuku<->Hakone, unlimited Hakone buses, Tozan Railway, Cable Car, Ropeway, and Lake Ashi pirate ship cruise. Children under 6 ride free (up to 2 per paying adult).

### The Hakone Loop (Classic Route)

| Step | Transport | Duration | Highlights |
|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| 1 | Romancecar to Hakone-Yumoto | 80 min | Scenic mountain approach |
| 2 | Tozan Railway to Gora | 40 min | Switchbacks through mountain forests |
| 3 | Cable Car to Sounzan | 10 min | Steep ascent with valley views |
| 4 | Ropeway to Togendai | 30 min | Owakudani volcanic valley, Fuji views |
| 5 | Pirate Ship across Lake Ashi | 30 min | Lake cruise with mountain backdrop |
| 6 | Bus back to Hakone-Yumoto | 35 min | Through Moto-Hakone area |

**Important:** At Owakudani, try the famous black eggs (kuro-tamago) boiled in volcanic hot springs -- 500 for 5 eggs. Legend says each egg adds 7 years to your life.

## #4 Mt Fuji / Kawaguchiko -- "The Icon"

**Why #4:** Nothing beats seeing Mt Fuji up close. October offers good visibility and the beginning of autumn foliage around the lake. The Kawaguchiko Autumn Leaves Festival may begin in late October (usually mid-November peak, but early color starts mid-October).

**Book bus tickets in advance** -- they sell out, especially on weekends.

| Saiko Iyashi no Sato | 500 (child 250) | Reconstructed thatched-roof village |

If you want to keep costs down, prioritize these:

|-------------|---------------|
| **Yokohama** | 30 min away, low train cost, free entry Chinatown/waterfront, pay-per-ride park |
| **Kamakura** | Modest train cost, many free shrines, beach is free |

### Tier 2 -- 500-700 NIS for a family of 4
| Destination | Main Cost Driver |
|-------------|-----------------|
| **Nikko** | Train + shrine admission |
| **Kawaguchiko** | Bus fare + ropeway |
| **Hakone** | Freepass is good value but higher absolute cost |

### Free & Low-Cost Highlights Anywhere
- Walking shrine/temple grounds (most are free to enter the grounds)
- Parks and gardens
- Street food instead of sit-down restaurants
- Conbini (convenience store) meals -- surprisingly excellent in Japan, 500-800 per person
- Pack onigiri and drinks from 7-Eleven for the day trip

## Practical Tips for All Day Trips

1. **IC Card (Suica/Pasmo):** Load money and tap to pay on all trains and buses. Works across all recommended destinations except some Hakone Loop transports (use Freepass instead).

2. **Timing:** Leave Tokyo by 8:00 AM at the latest. Most temples/attractions close at 4:30-5:00 PM.

3. **October weather:** Expect 15-23C. Bring a light jacket. Mountain areas (Hakone, Nikko, Kawaguchiko) are 3-5 degrees cooler.

4. **Kids:** Bring snacks and water. Trains are clean and punctual. Most stations have elevators and accessible bathrooms.

5. **Coin lockers:** Available at all major stations (300-700 depending on size). Store bags while you explore.

6. **Note on Sukkot (Oct 1-4):** If observing holiday restrictions on Oct 1-2 (Shemini Atzeret/Simchat Torah in Diaspora), avoid scheduling day trips on those dates. Kamakura or Yokohama could work well on Oct 5 (first free weekday) or on weekends.
