## Why This Version Is Better

- The children’s interests control the route.
- Shopping districts are time-boxed and include actual play.
- Todoroki Ravine adds a green, low-pressure reset after six dense city/fandom days.
- Osaka Castle, Golden Gai, Omoide Yokocho, the Tsukiji/Ginza checklist and repetitive viewpoints are gone.
- Ghibli now shares a focused western-Tokyo day rather than blocking Kyoto’s strongest festival date.
- Oct 9 now stays entirely in Tokyo for parks, Ghibli-shaped sweets and a fixed-date street-food festival.
- Two Kyoto nights add geiko dance, a shrine procession, samurai-film play and live taiko without adding a temple marathon.
- A short Fushimi Inari lower-gates walk adds one iconic Kyoto sight without creating another sightseeing day.
- Taiko-kan and a Miyamoto bachi turn the final Asakusa block into a personal drumming experience.
- Nintendo Museum is included on the only logical date, subject to its lottery.
- UZUMASA and HIBIKI form the main traditional-family day. UZUMASA is booked and paid at JPY 29,600 — yokai-festival tickets, a 13:30 tea ceremony and two ninja-attraction coupons, all valid only on Oct 12; Mibu Kyogen and a private samurai lesson are no longer possible add-ons.
