## 1. Flights

### Recommended: Ethiopian Airlines via Addis Ababa (CHEAPEST)
- **Route**: TLV → ADD (4.5h) → NRT (12h)
- **Price**: ~$600–700 per person round trip
- **Family total**: ~$2,400–2,800
- **Baggage**: 2x 23kg checked bags included (generous!)
- **Layover**: 3–10 hours

### Alternative: Turkish Airlines via Istanbul
- **Route**: TLV → IST (3.75h) → NRT (11.5h)
- **Price**: ~$700–850 per person
- **Family total**: ~$2,800–3,400
- **Baggage**: 1x 23kg checked bag

### Premium Option: EL AL Direct
- **Route**: TLV → NRT nonstop (11.5h)
- **Price**: ~$620+ one-way per person
- **Schedule**: Sun/Tue/Fri at 19:01, arrives ~12:30 next day
- **Pros**: Direct, Hebrew-speaking crew, familiar airline
- **Cons**: Most expensive option for round trip

### Where to Search & Book
1. **Google Flights** — google.com/travel/flights (best interface, price tracking)
2. **Skyscanner** — skyscanner.com (finds cheapest combos)
3. **Kayak** — kayak.com (price alerts)
4. **Direct airline sites** — often have exclusive deals
5. **Israeli agencies** — Issta, Ophir Tours (may have group discounts)

### Money-Saving Tips
- Book 4–6 months ahead (April–June 2026)
- Check Sep 30 evening departure (red-eye can save $50–200/person)
- Fly into Narita (NRT) — cheaper flights, airport transit is affordable
- Track prices on Google Flights

## 2. Accommodation

### RECOMMENDED: Airbnb in Ikebukuro (Tokyo base)

**Why Ikebukuro is perfect for your family:**
- Major transport hub (8 train lines!) — get anywhere fast
- Home to **Pokemon Center Mega Tokyo** (Sunshine City)
- **Animate flagship store** (9 floors of anime!)
- **Otome Road** (daughter will love this)
- Cheaper than Shinjuku/Shibuya
- Tons of budget restaurants

**Expected price**: $70–95/night for 2BR apartment
**10 nights total**: ~$700–950

### Alternative Budget Options
| Area | Price/night | Pros | Cons |
|------|-----------|------|------|
| **Asakusa** | $65–85 | Traditional, tourist-friendly, near Senso-ji | Touristy by day |
| **Ueno** | $60–80 | Cheapest central, near park/museums | Less trendy |
| **Sumida** | $55–75 | Near Skytree, very local | Fewer restaurants |

### Osaka Side Trip (2–3 nights)
**Toyoko Inn or Super Hotel in Namba**: $80–100/night
- Central to Dotonbori food street
- Easy access to USJ and Kyoto

### What to Look for on Airbnb
- Filter: "Entire place", 2+ bedrooms, 4 guests
- Must have: Washer/dryer, pocket WiFi, near station (5 min walk)
- Check: Registration number (legal requirement in Japan)
- Book by: June 2026 for best selection

### Search URLs
- Airbnb: search Ikebukuro/Toshima, Oct 1–17, 2BR, 4 guests
- Booking.com: filter family rooms, free cancellation
- Hostelworld: private family rooms (Khaosan Tokyo ~$60–80/night)

## 7. Transport Guide

### IC Cards (Suica/Pasmo)
- Buy **Welcome Suica** at airport (¥1,000 or ¥2,000, includes balance)
- Works on ALL trains, buses, convenience stores, vending machines
- Top up at any station or 7-Eleven
- One card per person

### Tokyo Trains
- Use IC card for all trips — no need for day passes
- Average ride: ¥170–320
- Daily budget: ~¥1,000/person for 4–5 rides
- Child fare (6–11): 50% — but your 12-year-old pays adult fare

### Airport Transfer
- **Keisei Access Express**: ¥1,270/person, 60–70 min to central Tokyo — BEST VALUE
- Alternative: Keisei Skyliner ¥2,520/person, 41 min to Ueno

### Tokyo → Osaka
- **Overnight bus (Willer Express)**: ¥5,000/person, 7–8 hours overnight
- Book at: willerexpress.com
- 4 passengers round trip: ~¥40,000 ($267)
- vs Shinkansen: ¥110,960 ($740) — saves $473!
